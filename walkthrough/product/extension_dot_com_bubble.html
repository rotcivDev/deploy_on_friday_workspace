Extension Outline: The .com Bubble

Patch type: startup-hype extension. Compatibility tags: startup, business, high-chaos, web.

Theme Promise

Players chase impossible growth, weird business models, domain-name gold rushes, banner ads, vaporware demos, and sudden market collapse.

New Content Direction

  • Monsters: Burn Rate Hydra, Portal Strategy, Banner Ad Revenue Model, IPO Deadline.
  • Equipment: Aeron Chair, Free Pizza Budget, Perl CGI Script, Company Hoodie.
  • Curses: Stock Options Underwater, Pivot Again, Domain Squatter.
  • Events: Launch Party, Investor Demo, Acquisition Rumor, Market Correction.
  • Achievements: Exit Before Profit, Survive The Crash, Win With Only Hype Cards.

Mechanic Outline

Use a valuation keyword that temporarily boosts Influence and Power but can collapse when specific Event or Curse cards resolve.

Deck Recipe Impact

  • Raises volatility and negotiation pressure.
  • Adds strong short-term Equipment with risky drawbacks.
  • Mixes well with The SaaS Bubble for business-model escalation.

Release Gate

  • Valuation bonuses are visible in command results.
  • Crash effects do not permanently corrupt base player stats.
  • Fixed-seed mixed decks produce repeatable boom-and-bust transcripts.
