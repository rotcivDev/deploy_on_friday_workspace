Extension Outline: The SaaS Bubble

Patch type: recurring-revenue operations extension. Compatibility tags: saas, cloud, business, infrastructure.

Theme Promise

Players enter the age of dashboards, subscriptions, churn, customer success, cloud bills, feature flags, observability, and every product somehow becoming a platform.

New Content Direction

  • Monsters: Churn Monster, Enterprise Sales Request, Multi-Tenant Incident, Runaway Cloud Bill.
  • Equipment: Observability Stack, Feature Flag System, Customer Success Script, Kubernetes Cluster.
  • Curses: Pager Rotation, Pricing Page Rewrite, Compliance Questionnaire.
  • Events: Renewal Season, Product-Led Growth Experiment, Status Page Update.
  • Achievements: Retain The Enterprise Whale, Defeat Runaway Cloud Bill, Win During Pager Duty.

Mechanic Outline

Use recurring effects that trigger at start of turn for a limited number of turns. Keep duration counters explicit and deterministic.

Deck Recipe Impact

  • Adds infrastructure and business pressure to the main deck.
  • Pairs naturally with The .com Bubble and The AI New Era.
  • Needs careful balance because recurring effects can snowball.

Release Gate

  • Recurring effects expire correctly in fixed-seed simulations.
  • Cloud-bill penalties cannot eliminate a player from the match immediately.
  • Deck validation flags recipes with too many recurring cards.
