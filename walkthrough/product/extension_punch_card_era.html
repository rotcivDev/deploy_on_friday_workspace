Extension Outline: The Punch Card Era

Patch type: historical retro extension. Compatibility tags: retro, hardware, low-level, career.

Theme Promise

Players face the physical constraints and ritual discipline of early computing: punch cards, mainframes, batch windows, operator queues, scarce memory, and one typo that ruins the whole run.

New Content Direction

  • Monsters: Dropped Card Stack, Mainframe Queue, One Character Typo, Vacuum Tube Failure.
  • Equipment: Card Sorter, Operator Badge, Assembly Handbook, Lab Coat.
  • Curses: Reorder The Deck By Hand, Batch Window Missed, Coffee On The Cards.
  • Events: Night Shift At The Mainframe, Printout Oracle, Memory Budget Review.
  • Achievements: Survive Batch Processing, Win With No Modern Tools, Defeat Mainframe Queue.

Mechanic Outline

Use a deterministic batch keyword: some cards delay rewards or penalties until the next turn. Keep it as a card-effect flag, not a new phase engine.

Deck Recipe Impact

  • Adds low-to-mid power Encounter cards with high annoyance effects.
  • Adds Treasure that rewards careful, low-chaos play.
  • Mixes well with The AI New Era for old-machine-versus-new-hype comedy.

Release Gate

  • Main deck still validates alone.
  • Mixed main plus Punch Card deck has at least one Monster, Equipment, Curse, and Event from each selected pack.
  • Batch effects are reproducible in fixed-seed simulation transcripts.
